Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ayva Labs Limited (“PawMe,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit pawmebot.com, reserve a PawMe device, or otherwise interact with our services.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact information — email address, and any name you choose to provide when reserving a PawMe device or joining our VIP list.
  • Order information — if you complete a reservation, our payment processor (Stripe) collects payment-card information directly. We receive a transaction confirmation, your email, and the amount; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Device and usage data — IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URL, pages viewed, and timestamps. Collected via our website analytics and advertising tools.
  • Behavioral analytics — through Microsoft Clarity we record mouse movement, clicks, scrolls, and page navigation so we can improve site usability. Clarity masks personal text by default.
  • Advertising data — through the Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API we share hashed identifiers (such as a hashed email and event identifiers) with Meta to measure ad performance.

2. How we use information

  • To process reservations, send confirmations, and provide product updates.
  • To run and improve the website and customer experience.
  • To measure and optimise marketing campaigns, including conversion attribution on Meta (Facebook and Instagram).
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud and abuse.

3. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Contract — to fulfil your reservation and related communications.
  • Legitimate interests — to run analytics, secure our service, and measure marketing in a privacy-conscious way.
  • Consent — for non-essential cookies and marketing pixels, where required.

4. Sharing of information

We share information only with service providers acting on our behalf:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Kit (ConvertKit) — email list management and broadcast.
  • Meta Platforms — ad delivery and conversion measurement.
  • Microsoft Clarity — behavioural analytics.
  • n8n Cloud — our automation backend that orchestrates the above.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

5. International transfers

Our providers process data in the United States, the European Economic Area, and other regions. Where required, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards.

6. Data retention

We keep reservation and order records for as long as needed to fulfil the order, provide warranty/customer support, and meet legal and accounting obligations (typically up to 7 years). Analytics data is retained according to each provider’s standard window (e.g., Meta retains ad-event data for up to two years).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Port your data to another service.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

To exercise any right, email pawme@ayvalabs.com. You can also follow the steps on our Data Deletion page to request removal of your data.

8. Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies (including the Meta Pixel and Clarity script) to operate the site, analyse usage, and measure advertising. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may affect functionality.

9. Children

PawMe is intended for adults (18+) who care for pets. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use TLS in transit, restrict access to personal data on a need-to-know basis, and rely on reputable processors that maintain industry-standard security controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
