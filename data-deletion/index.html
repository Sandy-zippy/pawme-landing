Data Deletion

Last updated: May 11, 2026

You have the right to ask Ayva Labs Limited to delete the personal data we hold about you. This page explains exactly how.

What we delete on request

  • Your email address and any name on file.
  • Your reservation record and order history (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Your subscription on our email platform (Kit).
  • Where technically possible, behavioural analytics and advertising identifiers linked to your contact information.

Some records, such as payment receipts and accounting entries, must be retained by law (typically up to 7 years). After that period they are deleted on a rolling basis.

How to request deletion

Send an email to pawme@ayvalabs.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request” and include:

  • The email address you used to reserve or sign up.
  • Your full name, if you provided one.
  • (Optional) Any reservation reference number.

We will confirm receipt within 2 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days, as required under GDPR, UK GDPR, and similar privacy laws.

Facebook / Meta data deletion

PawMe’s Facebook app is used solely to publish ads from our own account. We do not receive Facebook profile information beyond what is needed to authenticate our team. If you used Facebook Login or interacted with our Facebook ad in a way that left identifiers on our systems, the same email request above will trigger deletion of any matched record.

What to expect after deletion

  • You will no longer receive emails from PawMe.
  • Your reservation will be cancelled and any refundable deposit returned.
  • We will issue a written confirmation once deletion is complete. You can re-subscribe later if you wish.

Questions

For anything else, email pawme@ayvalabs.com.
