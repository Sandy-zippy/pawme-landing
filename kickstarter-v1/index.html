Will my card be charged immediately?+

No. Kickstarter is all-or-nothing — your card is only charged if PawMe hits its $5K funding goal by July 9, 2026. You can adjust or cancel your pledge anytime before then.

What about subscription fees?+

Will it work for cats?+

Yes. PawMe works with cats out of the box — same behavioral monitoring, same following. The Cat Pack stretch goal at $300K adds laser-play, cat-toy mode, and low-noise paw guards specific to cats.

Will my pet be scared of it?+

PawMe runs whisper-quiet wheels (under 35 dB — quieter than a refrigerator). Our 6-month pet-test program with 1,000+ dogs and cats showed 92% acclimated within 3 days. The onboarding mode introduces PawMe gradually, parking it stationary for the first 48 hours.

Furbo and Petcube are stationary cameras with treat-toss — they watch but don't follow, don't analyze behavior, and charge $7-13/mo with no founder-rate option. US, Canada, UK, EU, Australia, Hong Kong, Singapore. Other regions add $40 shipping. Customs/duties paid by backer (varies by country, typically $20-40).

What if it breaks?+
